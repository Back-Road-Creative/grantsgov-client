# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people.
- Assuming good faith, and asking before assuming otherwise.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers — use GitHub's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form on this repository, which is the
private channel available here, or GitHub's own
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow. Reports are
reviewed and investigated, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this policy, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
